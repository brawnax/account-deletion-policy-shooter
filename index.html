Privacy & Data Deletion Policy

Bubble Shooter 3D: Pop Master is a casual puzzle game developed as a free app. This service is provided at no cost and is intended for use as is. This page is used to inform users regarding our policies with the collection, use, disclosure, and deletion of Personal Information.

1. Information Collection and Use

Our game does not have an account registration or login system. We do not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number. However, the app uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device or advertising profile for marketing and functionality purposes.

2. Data Deletion Policy & User Rights

We respect user privacy and provide a clear mechanism to request data deletion, complying with Google Play's policies:

A. Account & Personal Data:

Since this game features no login functionality, there are no cloud accounts or personal databases hosted by us to delete. No personal identifiers are saved on our servers.

B. Third-Party Device & Advertising IDs:

Third-party networks like Google AdMob collect temporary device IDs and advertising identifiers. Users can delete or reset this data directly from their Android device by navigating to:
Settings → Google → Ads → Reset/Delete Advertising ID.

C. How to Request Deletion via Email [2]:

If you have any concerns regarding data tracking or want to request a complete purge of any residual data, you can contact us directly by email:

Whenever you use our Game, in case of an error or crash, data and information (through third-party products) is recorded on your phone called Log Data. This may include information such as your device Internet Protocol (“IP”) address, device name, operating system version, the configuration of the app, and other performance statistics.

4. Security

We value your trust in providing us access to technical identifiers, and we strive to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. But remember that no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

5. Children’s Privacy

These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided personal data, we immediately delete it. If you are a parent or guardian and realize your child has sent us details, please contact us.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy and Data Deletion Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
